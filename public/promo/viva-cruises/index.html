MS VIVA ENJOY

Launched in 2024, MS VIVA ENJOY is VIVA Cruises' newest addition. With 95 cabins across 3 decks, she carries just 190 guests for an intimate river cruise experience.

Choose from four cabin types: Emerald (panoramic window), Ruby (French balcony), Diamond (French balcony), or Suite Diamond (24m²). Every cabin includes a Nespresso machine, RITUALS products, daily-restocked minibar, and complimentary Wi-Fi.
