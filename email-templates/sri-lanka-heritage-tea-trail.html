One island, and almost everything you would want from Asia. Climb the Lion Rock at Sigiriya, stand in the cave temples at Dambulla, ride the hill train up through the tea gardens to Nuwara Eliya, then finish with three unhurried nights on the sand at Koggala. Three UNESCO World Heritage Sites, a private air-conditioned car and your own English-speaking chauffeur throughout — and dinner is already taken care of, every night.

Your Itinerary

10 days from the cultural triangle to the south coast
Day 1 Sigiriya — Met at Colombo airport, private transfer, meal on arrival
Day 2 Sigiriya — Lion Rock, village house visit & bullock cart ride
Day 3 Dambulla — Cave temple, then a private jeep safari in Minneriya
Day 4 Matale & Kandy — Spice gardens, head and shoulder massage, on to Kandy
Day 5 Kandy — Temple of the Tooth Relic & evening cultural show
Day 6 Nuwara Eliya — The hill-country train ride up through the tea gardens
Day 7 Koggala — Down to the coast, stopping for the turtles at Bentota
Day 8 Koggala — At leisure on the south coast
Day 9 Koggala — Golden sand, and the chance of the Green Flash at sunset
Day 10 Homeward — Private transfer to Colombo for your flight home
