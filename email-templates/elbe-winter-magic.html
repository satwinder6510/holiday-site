Two Christmas markets in one short cruise. You board in Prague for the Old Town markets, then spend a full day in Dresden at the Striezelmarkt, one of the oldest Christmas markets in Germany.

MS Elbe Princesse is a small paddlewheel boat built for the shallow Elbe. Meals, drinks with meals and at the bar, wi-fi and port fees are all included on board.

Your Itinerary

5 days, Prague to Prague
Day 1 Prague Fly from the UK, transfer to the ship, board at 6pm, welcome cocktail and dinner
Day 2 Prague Castle District in the morning, Old Town and the Christmas markets after lunch, concert on board
Day 3 Melnik Morning sailing to where the Elbe meets the Vltava, Nelahozeves Castle, gala dinner
Day 4 Dresden Full day in the city, lunch at a local restaurant, free time at the Striezelmarkt, rejoin the ship in Prague
Day 5 Prague Breakfast on board, transfer to the airport, fly home

The Prague, Nelahozeves and Dresden excursions are optional and bookable on board.
