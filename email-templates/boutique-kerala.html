This is the classic first Kerala journey: Kochi, the tea hills of Munnar, the spice country around Thekkady and the backwaters at Kumarakom. What changes is where you sleep. We have swapped the usual chain hotels for five places with a story of their own.

Your first night is on the River Periyar, half an hour from the airport. In Munnar you have a Pool Villa with its own solar-heated infinity pool. At Thekkady you sleep under elephant-grass thatch beside the tiger reserve. You finish at Coconut Lagoon, reached only by boat, in a 150-year-old Kerala house rebuilt beside the canals.
