Fly to Bordeaux, spend a night at the Seeko'o Hotel in the city centre, then board MS Cyrano de Bergerac for five nights on the Garonne, the Dordogne and the Gironde estuary. You sail past the Médoc vineyards, stop at Blaye for Vauban's citadel, and reach Libourne for the medieval village of Saint-Émilion. Every meal on board is included, and so are drinks at the bar, a welcome cocktail and a gala dinner. Departures in September and October, when the harvest is in.
